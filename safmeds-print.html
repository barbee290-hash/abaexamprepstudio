Printing tips

  • Index cards: in the print dialog, choose paper size 3×6 in (landscape), scale 100% / Actual size, and margins None.
  • Test first: print one or two cards before the whole deck to check alignment.
  • No double-sided printing? Print Fronts only, put the printed cards back in the tray, then print Backs only.
  • Regular paper: print double-sided (flip on long edge), then cut along the dashed lines.

You can also choose Save as PDF in the print dialog to keep a digital copy.
