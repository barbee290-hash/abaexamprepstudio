Free RBT Practice Test: 15 Sample Questions

Try 15 free sample questions covering all six sections of the 2026 RBT® exam. Choose an answer, then check the correct answer and a BCBA’s explanation. No sign-up needed.

15 questions All 6 exam sections Every answer explained
  1. Question 1 of 15 · Section A: Data Collection & Graphing

    During a 30-minute classroom observation, two Registered Behavior Technicians (RBTs) independently observe a student and record every occurrence of hand-raising. At the end of the session, RBT A calculates a total of 12 instances, while RBT B calculates a total of 14 instances. They subsequently divide the smaller total by the larger total and multiply by 100 to yield an 85.7% agreement score. Which of the following best identifies what the RBTs were evaluating through this specific calculation procedure?

    Show answer & explanation

    Correct answer: B. Interobserver agreement (IOA) using total count IOA

    Two observers independently recorded the same behavior, then divided the smaller total by the larger total and multiplied by 100 (12 ÷ 14 × 100 = 85.7%). That is total count IOA, which shows how consistently two people measured the behavior and how believable the data are. It does not measure whether the plan was followed (treatment integrity), how often the behavior happened over time (rate), or whether the goal matters to the student and family (social validity).

  2. Question 2 of 15 · Section A: Data Collection & Graphing

    On a line graph, the data points jump between very high and very low values from session to session, with no clear upward or downward direction. This pattern shows:

    Show answer & explanation

    Correct answer: C. High variability

    Variability describes how much data points differ from one another. Large session-to-session swings with no overall direction mean high variability, which makes it harder to tell whether an intervention is working.

  3. Question 3 of 15 · Section A: Data Collection & Graphing

    An RBT is reviewing a line graph showing a client’s daily frequency of elopement across Baseline and Intervention phases. During the session between Day 10 (the last day of Baseline) and Day 11 (the first day of Intervention), a major medication change occurred. On the graph, the RBT notes a solid vertical line separating Day 10 and Day 11, with data points on Day 10 and Day 11 connected by a solid line. Which of the following errors was made when constructing this graph?

    Show answer & explanation

    Correct answer: B. The data path should NOT connect across a phase change line.

    Why B is correct: A fundamental convention of ABA line graphs is that data paths are broken across phase change lines (or condition change lines). Connecting data points across phases falsely implies continuous data collection under the same conditions.

    Why A is incorrect: Solid vertical lines are standard for major phase changes (for example, baseline to intervention), whereas dashed vertical lines are used for minor condition changes (for example, changing the prompt level within an intervention).

    Why C is incorrect: Environmental or external changes, like medication adjustments, are often annotated on graphs using condition change lines to give context to shifts in the data.

    Why D is incorrect: Data points within the same phase or condition are standardly connected to show trends over time.

  4. Question 4 of 15 · Section B: Behavior Assessment

    Your supervisor asks you to present items from a skills checklist and record whether the learner responds independently, so they can choose teaching goals. What are you helping with?

    Show answer & explanation

    Correct answer: B. A skill assessment

    Presenting skill items and recording independent responses is part of assessing skill strengths and deficits. The supervisor then uses the results to select teaching goals.

  5. Question 5 of 15 · Section C: Behavior Acquisition

    A client who hasn’t had a snack for three hours works much harder than usual to earn pretzels. The three hours without food is an example of:

    Show answer & explanation

    Correct answer: D. An establishing operation

    An establishing operation is a motivating operation that temporarily increases the value of a reinforcer. Going without food makes pretzels more valuable, so the client works harder to earn them.

  6. Question 6 of 15 · Section C: Behavior Acquisition

    Which type of prompt is usually considered the most intrusive?

    Show answer & explanation

    Correct answer: C. Full physical prompt

    Full physical guidance gives the most assistance, so it is the most intrusive. Prompting procedures fade prompts in a planned way to build independence.

  7. Question 7 of 15 · Section C: Behavior Acquisition

    A client now completes a skill reliably, and the plan says to “thin the schedule of reinforcement.” What does that mean?

    Show answer & explanation

    Correct answer: A. Gradually reinforce the skill less often

    Thinning means gradually moving from continuous to intermittent reinforcement. This helps the skill hold up in everyday settings, where reinforcement isn’t delivered every time.

  8. Question 8 of 15 · Section C: Behavior Acquisition

    What best distinguishes natural environment teaching (NET) from discrete-trial teaching (DTT)?

    Show answer & explanation

    Correct answer: B. NET is built into the learner’s everyday activities and follows their interests

    NET embeds teaching in natural routines and play, using the learner’s own motivation. DTT is more structured, with short, repeated trials usually led by the adult.

  9. Question 9 of 15 · Section D: Behavior Reduction

    A client bangs on the table only when a sibling is holding the tablet, and the banging stops once the client is given the tablet. What is the most likely function of the behavior?

    Show answer & explanation

    Correct answer: A. Access to tangibles

    The behavior happens when an item is unavailable and stops once the item is delivered, so it is most likely maintained by access to that tangible.

  10. Question 10 of 15 · Section D: Behavior Reduction

    After a client swears, the caregiver removes 10 minutes of video game time, and swearing decreases over the next few weeks. This is an example of:

    Show answer & explanation

    Correct answer: C. Negative punishment

    Something the client values (game time) was removed after the behavior, and the behavior decreased. Removing a stimulus to decrease behavior is negative punishment.

  11. Question 11 of 15 · Section D: Behavior Reduction

    A behavior that stopped during extinction suddenly reappears briefly at the start of a session several days later. This is called:

    Show answer & explanation

    Correct answer: D. Spontaneous recovery

    Spontaneous recovery is the brief return of an extinguished behavior after time has passed. Keep following the extinction plan so the behavior isn’t reinforced again.

  12. Question 12 of 15 · Section E: Documentation & Reporting

    Midway through a session, you can’t remember whether the behavior plan allows a break after two tasks or three. What should you do?

    Show answer & explanation

    Correct answer: B. Check the written plan, and if it’s still unclear, contact your supervisor

    RBTs follow the written plan and seek clinical direction from their supervisor when something is unclear, rather than guessing or changing the procedure.

  13. Question 13 of 15 · Section E: Documentation & Reporting

    Which statement belongs in an objective session note?

    Show answer & explanation

    Correct answer: B. The client completed 12 of 15 tracing trials independently

    Objective notes report observable events and data. Labels, opinions, and guesses about causes don’t belong in session documentation.

  14. Question 14 of 15 · Section F: Ethics

    A friend asks which family you work with in their neighborhood. What is the best response?

    Show answer & explanation

    Correct answer: C. Politely explain that you can’t share information about clients

    Confidentiality means not sharing any identifying client information with people who aren’t authorized to have it, even casually and even without names.

  15. Question 15 of 15 · Section F: Ethics

    Your supervisor has been out, and you realize you haven’t received the supervision required this month. What should you do?

    Show answer & explanation

    Correct answer: B. Tell your supervisor or RBT Requirements Coordinator right away so required supervision is arranged

    RBTs may practice only while receiving the ongoing supervision the BACB® requires. Report the gap promptly so it can be fixed, and never falsify supervision records.
