The RBT exam at a glance

85total questions
75scored questions
10unscored pilot items
90minutes

All questions are multiple choice. The 10 pilot questions are mixed in with the scored ones and don’t count toward your result, but you won’t know which ones they are, so treat every question as if it counts.

RBT requirements changed in 2026. Always confirm the latest eligibility, application, and exam details in the official RBT Handbook (opens in a new tab) before you schedule your exam.

Section A: Data Collection and Graphing 13 questions · 17%

Measurement, graphing, operational definitions, and reliable data. Here is what each task covers, in plain language:

  • A.1Continuous measurement: frequency, duration, latency, and interresponse time
  • A.2Discontinuous measurement: partial interval, whole interval, and momentary time sampling
  • A.3Permanent product recording
  • A.4Entering data and updating graphs
  • A.5Describing behavior and the environment in observable, measurable terms
  • A.6Calculating and summarizing data: rate, mean duration, and percentage
  • A.7Identifying trends in graphed data
  • A.8Risks of unreliable data and poor procedural fidelity

  • C.5Chaining: forward, backward, and total task

  • C.7Stimulus and response prompts, and fading them
  • C.8Generalization across settings, people, and stimuli
  • C.9Maintenance versus acquisition
  • C.10Shaping

Study tips that actually work

  • Study by weight. Sections C and D make up 44% of the exam together. Give them the most time.
  • Learn the “why”. Read every explanation, even for questions you got right. Scenario questions test understanding, not memorization.
  • Know the vocabulary cold. Positive vs. negative, reinforcement vs. punishment, and DRA vs. DRI vs. DRO appear again and again.
  • Practice under time pressure. Use Exam mode to get comfortable at a little over one minute per question.
  • Think like an RBT. When in doubt, the best answer usually involves following the plan, protecting the client, and checking with your supervisor.
  • Space it out. Short daily sessions beat one long cram session.

Test-day checklist

  • Read Pearson VUE’s BACB exam page (opens in a new tab). RBT exams are taken in person at a Pearson VUE test center, and the page covers scheduling, fees, accepted ID, check-in rules, and accommodations.
  • Confirm your appointment time, location, and ID requirements the day before.
  • Get a good night’s sleep, and eat before you go.
  • Arrive early so you aren’t rushed.
  • Read every answer choice before choosing, and watch for words like first, best, and most appropriate.
  • Don’t get stuck. Mark your best guess and keep moving, since unanswered questions can’t earn points.

Quick answers

How many questions are on the RBT exam?

The RBT® exam has 85 multiple-choice questions: 75 scored questions and 10 unscored pilot questions. You have 90 minutes to complete it.

Which section of the RBT exam has the most questions?

Behavior Acquisition (Section C) is the largest, with 19 of the 75 scored questions (about 25%). Behavior Reduction is next, with 14 questions (about 19%).

When did the new RBT exam outline take effect?

The RBT® Test Content Outline (3rd ed.) applies to RBT exams beginning January 1, 2026. It replaced the RBT Task List (2nd ed.).
