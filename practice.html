Two ways to practice

Pick the mode that fits your study session

Study mode

See whether you’re right, with an explanation, after every question. Ideal when you’re learning a section for the first time or reviewing mistakes.

Exam mode

A 21-minute timer, the same pace as the real 85-question, 90-minute exam. Flag questions, jump around, and see your full results and review at the end.

Study tip: Take the mock exam first, then practice your two lowest sections in Study mode. Retake the mock exam in Exam mode once you’re scoring 80% or higher by section.
