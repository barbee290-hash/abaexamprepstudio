Privacy policy

Last updated September 26, 2026

The Stimulus Control Room (“we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect when you use thestimuluscontrolroom.com and how we use it.

Practice quizzes

You don’t need an account to use our quizzes. Your answers are processed in your own browser and are not sent to us. Your best score for each quiz is saved in your browser’s local storage so we can show it to you next time. You can clear it at any time by clearing your browser’s site data.

Feedback and waitlist forms

When you submit the feedback form or join the full-exam waitlist, the information you enter (such as your name, email address, rating, and message) is sent to us by email so we can read your feedback, reply if you asked us to, or notify you when full-length exams launch. Waitlist email addresses are also kept in a private list on our hosting account for the same purpose. We do not sell or share this information with third parties for marketing.

Cookies and analytics

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. To understand how many people visit and which pages are most useful, we use Cloudflare Web Analytics, a privacy-first service that does not use cookies, does not track you across other websites, and does not collect personal information. It records anonymous totals such as page views, the page you came from, and your general country, device, and browser type. Our website fonts are provided by Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google’s servers to load them. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and performance.

Children’s privacy

This site is intended for adults preparing for a professional certification. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or want us to delete information you sent? Email contact@abaexamprepstudio.com.
