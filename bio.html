Meet the founder

Jeanne Barbee, MS, BCBA, LBA

Founder and Owner of The Stimulus Control Room. Board Certified Behavior Analyst and Georgia Licensed Behavior Analyst with seven years of experience in applied behavior analysis.

contact@thestimuluscontrolroom.com

Background

From behavioral therapist to BCBA

I started in June 2019 as a behavioral therapist, implementing intervention plans and providing 1:1 teaching with discrete trial training and natural environment training. I earned my RBT credential later that year.

I have worked in applied behavior analysis for seven years, four of them as a BCBA since 2022. As a BCBA, I have designed and supervised individualized ABA programs for clients in clinic, center, and multiple community settings.

I have completed direct and indirect assessments, including the VB-MAPP, ABLLS-R, and Socially Savvy.

Credentials & education

  • MS, Applied Psychology — Sacred Heart University
  • BA, Psychology — Wesleyan College
  • Certificate, ABA Advanced Series — Florida Institute of Technology
  • BCBA — Board Certified Behavior Analyst (since 2022)
  • LBA — Georgia Licensed Behavior Analyst
  • RBT — Registered Behavior Technician (earned 2019)
Why I built this

Built by someone who studied for these exams

When I studied for the RBT and BCBA exams, I found few resources that explained the concepts well enough to really understand them. I kept researching across many websites just to piece the information together.

The Stimulus Control Room is the resource I wished existed. Every mock exam is written by a BCBA — me — and the feedback is written the way a supervisor gives it: direct, clear, and focused on understanding, not just the right answer.

I am building a site with learning materials for different learning styles. It starts with RBT exam prep, and it is designed for anyone preparing for certification — whether you are studying between shifts, on a lunch break, or at home the night before your exam.
