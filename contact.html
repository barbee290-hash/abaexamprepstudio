Free practice quizzes

Do I need to create an account?

No. All practice quizzes are free and open to everyone with no sign-up required. Your best score for each quiz is saved only in your own browser and is never sent to us.

How many questions are in each practice quiz?

Each quiz has 20 questions. The mock exam mixes questions from all six sections of the RBT® exam, and each section quiz focuses on one section at a time.

What’s the difference between Study mode and Exam mode?

Study mode shows whether you’re right — with a full explanation — after each question. Exam mode gives you 21 minutes, mirrors the real exam’s pace, and shows your results and a complete review at the end.

Will I see the same questions every time?

Answer choices are shuffled on every attempt. The free mock exam also draws a different mix of questions each time you take it, so repeated practice stays useful.

Are these the actual RBT exam questions?

No. All questions are written independently by our team to match the topics in the RBT® Test Content Outline (3rd ed.). They are never copied from the real exam.

Is passing these quizzes a guarantee I’ll pass the RBT exam?

No practice resource can guarantee a result. Consistent practice across every section, combined with your required RBT training and supervisor guidance, is the best way to prepare — but the outcome depends on many factors.

I think a question or answer is wrong. What should I do?

Please tell us! Use the feedback form and choose “Report an error,” or email contact@thestimuluscontrolroom.com. We review every report and correct errors quickly.

How do I receive my files after purchasing?

After a successful payment you will receive an email containing a secure download link. Files are typically available within a few minutes. If your link hasn’t arrived within 30 minutes, check your spam folder and then email us.

Can I get a refund?

Because our products are digital files that are available for download immediately after purchase, all sales are final once the download link has been accessed. However, if your file was not delivered, is corrupted, or you were charged in error, we will make it right. See our full Refund Policy.

What format are the files in?

All paid products are delivered as PDF files. They are formatted for both screen reading and printing, and work on any device — phone, tablet, or computer — without any special software beyond a free PDF reader.

Can I share the files with my study group?

Purchases are licensed for personal, non-commercial use only. Please do not share, redistribute, or post the files publicly. If your clinic or supervisor would like materials for a team, email us and we can discuss options.

I didn’t receive my download link. What should I do?

First, check your spam or junk folder. If it’s not there, email us at contact@thestimuluscontrolroom.com with your order confirmation number and we’ll resend the link promptly.
