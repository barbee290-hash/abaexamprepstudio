FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to create an account?

No. All practice quizzes are free and open to everyone. Your best score for each quiz is saved only in your own browser.

How many questions are in each practice quiz?

Each quiz has 20 questions. The mock exam mixes questions from all six sections, and each section quiz focuses on one section of the RBT® exam.

What’s the difference between Study mode and Exam mode?

Study mode shows whether you’re right, with an explanation, after each question. Exam mode gives you 21 minutes, the same pace as the real exam, and shows your results and a full review at the end.

Will I see the same questions every time?

Answer choices are shuffled on every attempt, and the free mock exam draws a different mix of questions each time you take it.

Are these the actual RBT exam questions?

No. All questions are written independently to match the topics in the RBT® Test Content Outline (3rd ed.). They are never copied from the real exam.

Is passing these quizzes a guarantee I’ll pass the RBT exam?

No practice resource can guarantee a result, but consistent practice across every section, along with your required RBT training and supervisor guidance, is a great way to prepare.

I think a question is wrong. What should I do?

Please tell us! Use the feedback form and choose “Report an error”, or email contact@abaexamprepstudio.com.

Is ABA Exam Prep Studio part of the BACB?

No. We are an independent exam preparation resource and are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the Behavior Analyst Certification Board® (BACB®). For official requirements, visit bacb.com.
