How it works

Four steps, one minute, every day

Shuffle

Pick a deck. Cards come in a new random order every timing, so you learn the terms, not the sequence.

Say it fast

Read the term and say the definition out loud in your own words, as quickly as you can.

Flip & sort

Flip to check. Tap Correct if you had it, or Error if you missed it, hesitated, or skipped.

Chart daily

Your best timing each day is saved and charted. Aim for more corrects and fewer errors over time.

Fluency tip: Being accurate isn’t enough on a timed exam. You want to be accurate and fast. Review the cards you missed after each timing, then try again tomorrow.

Questions

SAFMEDS FAQ

What does SAFMEDS stand for?

Say All Fast, Minute Every Day, Shuffled. It is a flashcard method from precision teaching for building fluency: answering accurately and quickly.

How do I do a SAFMEDS timing?

Shuffle the deck and start a one-minute timer. Read each term, say the definition out loud, flip the card to check, and sort it into correct or error. When the minute ends, count and chart both piles.

What counts as a correct answer?

Say the definition in your own words before you flip. If it matches the meaning on the back, it is correct. If you hesitate too long, skip it, or get it wrong, count it as an error.
