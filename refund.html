Refund Policy

Last updated October 29, 2026

This policy applies to all paid digital products sold by The Stimulus Control Room, LLC through this website.

Digital downloads — general rule

All of our products are delivered as downloadable PDF files. Because a digital file can be copied the moment it is downloaded, we do not offer refunds once a download link has been accessed. Please review the free sample pages available on each product page before purchasing to make sure the material is a good fit for you.

Exceptions we will always honor

We will issue a full refund or replacement without argument in any of the following situations:

  • File not delivered. You completed payment but never received a download link by email (and it is not in your spam folder) within 24 hours of purchase.
  • File is defective. The file you downloaded is corrupted, unreadable, or incomplete (e.g., missing pages).
  • Wrong product delivered. You received a file that does not match what you purchased.
  • Duplicate charge. Your payment method was charged more than once for the same order.

In any of these cases, email us at contact@thestimuluscontrolroom.com within 30 days of purchase and include your order confirmation number. We will respond within 2 business days.

How to request a refund

  1. Email contact@thestimuluscontrolroom.com with the subject line “Refund Request.”
  2. Include your name, the email address used at checkout, and your order confirmation number.
  3. Briefly describe the issue (e.g., “file not received,” “file is corrupted”).

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method. Processing time depends on your card issuer but is typically 5–10 business days after we approve the request.

Chargebacks

We encourage you to contact us directly before initiating a chargeback with your bank or card issuer. We are a small business and respond promptly; most issues can be resolved within one business day. Chargebacks filed without contacting us first may result in your email address being blocked from future purchases.

Free content

Our free practice quizzes, mock exams, and other no-cost tools are provided at no charge and are not subject to any purchase or refund terms.

Questions

If you have any questions about this policy or your purchase, email contact@thestimuluscontrolroom.com. We’re happy to help.
